WHO WE ARE

Creativity meets technology.

CRETA is a creative production, localisation and technology studio based in Kolkata. With 6+ years of experience, 400+ delivered projects and 300+ happy clients, we help OTT platforms, brands, creators and influencers tell better stories and build smarter businesses.

Our team brings together writers, translators, voice artists, editors, creative technologists and developers. We create AI micro dramas and ad films, localise content in 70+ Indian and international languages, produce voice-overs and audiobooks, and build AI assistants, SaaS tools and automations. Every engagement starts with a real brief and ends with human-reviewed work.
