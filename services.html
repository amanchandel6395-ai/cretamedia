AI film production: AI-powered. Human-directed.

A production pipeline for cinematic AI micro dramas, ad films and branded content—not just a collection of generated clips.

STEP 01
Brief & screenplay
Define the audience, offer, format and story. We develop hooks, screenplay, shot list and an approved treatment for a micro drama, ad film or campaign.
STEP 02
Visual development
Create character references, style frames and storyboards. Establish a visual bible to guide identity, wardrobe, locations and continuity.
STEP 03
AI shot production
Use Higgsfield, Google Flow, Kling and Runway as the brief requires. Generate and iterate shots, direct motion and check character and scene consistency.
STEP 04
Voice & sound
Cast human talent or use approved AI voices through tools such as ElevenLabs. Add dialogue, music and sound design, with consent for any cloned voice.
STEP 05
Edit & finishing
Editors shape pacing and story, composite and retouch shots, grade colour, add graphics and subtitles, and create campaign cutdowns.
STEP 06
Review & launch
Review cuts against milestones, make adjustments, prepare platform exports, and deliver masters with organised stems and project assets.
