DIALOGUE TRACKING & SYNC
Native voice casting, directed dubbing, and linguistic QC.

From script to screen.
In every language.

One coordinated pipeline, native-language expertise and human quality control at every stage.

STAGE 01
Script & assets
We receive your script, picture, reference audio and delivery specifications. We confirm target languages, glossary, character profiles and pronunciation.
STAGE 02
Translation & adaptation
Native-language specialists translate meaning and emotion, adapt cultural references and prepare dialogue timed for the performance and lip-sync.
STAGE 03
Casting & directed dubbing
Voice auditions match age, character and tone. Directors guide native artists through recording, pickups and performance consistency.
STAGE 04
Dialogue edit & mix
We clean and synchronise dialogue, balance voices with music and effects, and mix to the agreed platform loudness and delivery specification.
STAGE 05
Subtitles & accessibility
Time-coded subtitles, captions and on-screen text adaptation are prepared, with readability, timing and language consistency checked.
STAGE 06
QC & final masters
Linguistic, sync and technical reviews lead to corrections, approvals and organised delivery of masters, audio stems and subtitle files as agreed.
